# Char Cuterie
High school project (Planning: Oct-Nov 2026, Coding Dec 2026)

Being someone who likes to spend a few minutes of their day solving crosswords or "Wordle" inspired games, I wanted to create my own twist on the genre.
Of course, as this is my first game I've ever tackled to create, I was aiming to develop something that I would enjoy without it becoming a bore after 2 minutes. In addition, as someone who loves to doodle, I aimed to add a bit of my own artistic flair to my game!

Premise: You are a little mouse, Char (short for Charlie), in a 1915s-esque setting, and your rodent family was struck hard by the Great Cheese-pression. With the last cheese wheel you have to your name, you decide to open a little deli stand, specializing in--you guessed it--charcuterie boards!
Type the names of ingredients quickly and correctly to satisfy customers in a certain amount of time and aim for the highest score... while keeping your earnings above the rent!
